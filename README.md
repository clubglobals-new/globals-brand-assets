# GLOBALS brand asset pack v1.0 (2026-10-17)

Public mirror of the partner asset pack described in the GLOBALS Brand Manual v1.0 (globals.co/brand/, chapter 11).
Contents: `logos/svg` (20 vector marks: GLOBALS lockup in the four modes, knot alone, Club GLOBALS lockup and alternatives), `logos/png` (the same as 2000 px rasters), `diagrams` (clear space, minimum sizes, lock-up grammar, layout families, GLOBALS × The Social Hub, do-not; SVG + PNG + the generator), `colour` (palette JSON/CSV, swatch sheet, the three gradient tiles). `ASSETS-INDEX.md` lists provenance and verification per file.

Rules of use are in the manual. Partner marks on GLOBALS surfaces are black or white only; the gold-on-dark lockup is the primary mode. AI/EPS/PDF masters are referenced by Google Drive id in the manual's chapter 11; fonts come from Google Fonts (Urbanist 500..700).
