## 7. Known gaps (carried from the inventory, confirmed here)

- **No vector On Tour lockup, no light-background On Tour variant, no On Tour file in Drive at all** (repo PNG only).
- **GSTF is raster only** and the current PNGs hold the art at ~385×98 px inside a 496 px canvas: unusable above ~400 px wide. The 1200 px files carry the retired "GLOBAL Sales" name.
- **GLOBALS tv is raster only** and not reachable from this session (not link-shared).
- The GLOBALS wordmark "SVGs" in Drive are raster wrappers; the only true vector of the umbrella wordmark is the reconstruction in this pack.
- The BrandKit's three "DOWNLOAD HERE" links point at `1HWEAcsr0llQVy4K2Au-HO5LyvsEncsZ9`, which holds the Club GLOBALS sheet only — no GLOBALS wordmark, no fonts, no GSTF. **Stale.**
- The B1 "master" is a multi-logo sheet; the clean single lockup lives in the Vectors folder. The index names the right file for each job.
- Master-file inconsistencies to settle in the manual: "GLOBALS" ink `#231F20` vs "Club" `#000000` in the Illustrator file; wordmark text `#444444` on BrandKit p.2 vs `#000000` in the Drive PNG; knot petal values differ slightly between the vector (`#F66109`) and the BrandKit pixel sample (`#F26322`).
- Nothing in CMYK or Pantone except the (unreachable) glasses print file; the values here are naive conversions.
- No favicon/app-icon set from the 2025 knot was produced here (colour choice gold vs full-colour is a brand decision); `globals-knot-*.svg` are the sources once decided.
- Xcellerator / Jobs / Homes: Jobs and Homes lockups exist only inside the B1 sheet (`Logo_ClubGLOBALS.ai/.pdf`); no Xcellerator mark found.
